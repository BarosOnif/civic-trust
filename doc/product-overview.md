# CivicTrust — Product Overview

## Product

**CivicTrust — Evidence Before Confidence**

CivicTrust is an AI-assisted civic information and action platform designed to help people **find, understand, verify and act on civic information they can trust**.

The initial focus is Nigeria, with an architecture that can be extended to other locations and languages over time.

## Core Product Journey

CivicTrust is built around four connected actions:

**FIND → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → ACT**

### FIND

Help users locate relevant civic information, public institutions, documents, services and reporting pathways.

### UNDERSTAND

Translate complex civic information into clear, plain language so ordinary citizens can understand what it means, who it affects and what they need to know.

### VERIFY

Help users examine civic claims against available evidence and understand whether a claim is supported, contradicted, outdated, incomplete or unable to be verified.

### ACT

Help users identify an appropriate next step, responsible institution, reporting channel or other legitimate action.

## Core Product Principle

> **“We don't ask you to trust the AI. We show you the evidence.”**

CivicTrust is not designed to make the AI itself the source of authority. Instead, the platform should make relevant evidence and sources visible to the user.

Users should be able to understand:

* where information came from;
* who published it;
* when it was published or last checked;
* why a source was selected;
* what evidence supports the answer;
* what remains uncertain;
* what the user can do next.

## Core Features

### Ask CivicTrust

Users can ask civic questions using natural language and receive an understandable response based on available civic information and sources.

### Verify a Claim

Users can submit civic claims and receive a verification assessment supported by available evidence.

### Evidence & Sources

Users can inspect the sources and supporting information behind answers and verification results.

### Document Explainer

Users can submit or upload civic documents and receive an AI-assisted explanation of key points, important dates, requirements, affected groups and other relevant information.

### Civic Search & Explore

Users can explore civic information across categories such as:

* Government Services
* Laws & Policies
* Elections & Voting
* Public Institutions
* Your Rights
* Public Spending
* Education
* Transport
* Environment
* Business & Regulation
* Local Government
* Public Safety
* Civic Participation
* Community & Social Cohesion

### Report an Issue

Users can report civic, public-service, community and relevant safety concerns through a structured reporting flow.

### Safety & Protection

CivicTrust can provide safety-related reporting pathways and, where verified information is available, help users identify appropriate support or reporting channels.

### Community & Social Cohesion

CivicTrust supports community-related information, concerns and action pathways without becoming a social network or replacing professional mediation services.

### Civic Action / Next Step

Where evidence is available, CivicTrust helps users understand who may be responsible and what legitimate next step may be appropriate.

### Source Directory

Users can explore information about civic/public institutions and other source organisations.


## Intended User Experience

A typical CivicTrust interaction follows:

```text
QUESTION / CLAIM / ISSUE
          ↓
     UNDERSTAND REQUEST
          ↓
   FIND RELEVANT SOURCES
          ↓
      CHECK EVIDENCE
          ↓
     EXPLAIN CLEARLY
          ↓
    SHOW SOURCES/EVIDENCE
          ↓
    IDENTIFY NEXT STEP
```

## What CivicTrust Is Not

CivicTrust is not intended to be:

* a generic AI chatbot;
* a political campaign platform;
* a social media network;
* a news aggregation platform;
* a government website replacement;
* a professional emergency-response service;
* a replacement for legal, medical or other professional advice.

Its core role is to help people **navigate civic information with greater clarity, evidence and transparency**.

## Current Product Status

CivicTrust is a **working proof of concept** developed for an invention sprint.

The POC demonstrates the core idea and selected functional workflows rather than claiming to be a production-ready national civic infrastructure platform.
