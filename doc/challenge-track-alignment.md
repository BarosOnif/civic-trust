# CivicTrust — Challenge Track Alignment

CivicTrust is designed to address all three challenge tracks through one connected civic-information and action model.

The platform's **primary track is Transparency & Accountability**, while Safety, Reporting & Protection and Stability & Social Cohesion are important secondary and cross-track applications.

---

## 1. Transparency & Accountability

### Primary Track

CivicTrust directly addresses this track by helping people:

* find civic information;
* understand policies and public documents;
* verify civic claims;
* inspect supporting evidence;
* identify public institutions;
* understand civic procedures;
* access appropriate information and reporting pathways;
* report public-service and civic issues.

### Relevant CivicTrust Features

**Ask CivicTrust**

Allows users to ask civic questions in natural language.

**Document Explainer**

Helps users understand complex civic documents and policies.

**Verify a Claim**

Allows users to assess claims against available evidence.

**Evidence & Sources**

Makes supporting sources visible.

**Civic Search / Explore**

Helps users discover civic information and institutions.

**Report an Issue**

Provides a structured way to record civic/public-service concerns.

**Civic Action / Next Step**

Helps users identify responsible institutions and appropriate next actions.

### Track Statement

> CivicTrust improves civic transparency by making public information easier to find, understand, verify and act upon.

---

# 2. Safety, Reporting & Protection

### Secondary Track

CivicTrust extends its information and reporting model to relevant safety concerns.

The platform can support:

* safety-related reporting;
* public-safety concerns;
* anonymous reporting where appropriate;
* identification of appropriate reporting pathways;
* verified support information where available;
* clear distinctions between emergency assistance and non-emergency reporting.

### Important Limitation

CivicTrust is **not an emergency-response service**.

It must not claim to dispatch responders, contact authorities or provide emergency assistance unless a genuine, functioning integration exists.

Where verified emergency or support information is unavailable, the platform should communicate that limitation rather than inventing a contact or organisation.

### Track Statement

> CivicTrust provides citizens with clearer, privacy-conscious pathways for reporting relevant safety concerns and finding appropriate verified information and support pathways.

---

# 3. Stability & Social Cohesion

### Cross-Track

CivicTrust contributes to stability and social cohesion primarily through:

* trusted civic information;
* claim verification;
* reducing misunderstanding;
* helping people understand community issues;
* identifying responsible institutions;
* providing legitimate action pathways;
* supporting community concern reporting.

A citizen may encounter conflicting information about a local issue. CivicTrust can help the user:

**Find information → examine evidence → understand the context → identify an appropriate next step.**

### Important Limitation

CivicTrust is not designed to replace professional mediation or become a social network.

Its contribution to social cohesion comes through **better information, clearer context and appropriate civic action**.

### Track Statement

> CivicTrust can help reduce civic friction and misunderstanding by making reliable information easier to access, verify and understand while helping users navigate legitimate community and institutional pathways.

---

# Overall Positioning

### Primary

**Transparency & Accountability**

### Secondary

**Safety, Reporting & Protection**

### Cross-Track

**Stability & Social Cohesion**

All three tracks are connected through the central CivicTrust journey:
