# CivicTrust — Intended Users

## Primary Users

### Ordinary Citizens

CivicTrust is primarily designed for ordinary citizens who need to interact with civic information but may not have specialist knowledge of government, law or public administration.

They may be people who:

* need reliable civic information;
* want to understand a government service or policy;
* receive a civic claim through WhatsApp, social media or another channel;
* want to verify information before relying on it;
* need to identify a responsible public institution;
* want to understand an official document;
* need help identifying an appropriate next step;
* want to report a civic or public-service concern;
* need information about a relevant safety or community issue.

## Secondary Users

CivicTrust may also provide value to:

### Journalists

For finding, understanding and cross-checking civic information and source material.

### Researchers

For exploring civic information, documents, institutions and evidence.

### Civil-Society Organisations

For understanding public information and supporting citizen-facing work.

### Community Organisations

For navigating community concerns and identifying appropriate civic channels.

### Accountability Organisations

For monitoring public information, institutional decisions and reported civic issues.

### Public-Sector Service Teams

Potentially, in future, for receiving structured citizen concerns and improving access to public information.

## Citizen-First Principle

Although several groups can benefit from CivicTrust, the platform is fundamentally **citizen-first**.

The experience should remain understandable to someone who:

* is not a policy expert;
* may have limited time;
* may be using a mobile phone;
* may have limited bandwidth;
* may not know which institution is responsible.

## Access Model

The public POC should minimise unnecessary barriers to entry.

Core civic-information functions should be accessible without requiring an account wherever practical.

Optional accounts may support future functionality such as:

* saved information;
* question history;
* report tracking;
* personal preferences.

Administrative functionality should remain protected.

## User Need

The common user need can be expressed as:

> **“Help me understand what is happening, show me what I can verify, and tell me what I can do next.”**
