# CivicTrust — Future Development

CivicTrust is currently a **proof of concept**. Future development would focus on improving source coverage, access, safety, local relevance and action pathways.

## 1. Broader Verified Sources

Future versions could include:

* wider government source coverage;
* structured public records;
* official APIs;
* public-document monitoring;
* source freshness monitoring;
* automated change detection.

## 2. Government and Service Integrations

Potential future integrations include:

* verified government service directories;
* official complaint routing;
* public-service status information;
* structured service requirements;
* local-government information.

These should only be implemented through genuine, verified integrations.

## 3. Safety and Protection

Future development could strengthen:

* verified safety-resource directories;
* support organisations;
* clearer emergency/non-emergency pathways;
* privacy-preserving reporting;
* location-aware information where appropriate and safe.

## 4. Community and Social Cohesion

Potential development areas include:

* community-information hubs;
* local issue navigation;
* conflict-prevention information;
* verified community resources;
* stronger pathways for community concerns.

CivicTrust should not position itself as a professional mediation service unless such a capability is separately developed and governed.

## 5. Language Expansion

The initial interface is designed around English.

Future language support could include:

* Yoruba;
* Hausa;
* Igbo;
* Nigerian Pidgin.

## 6. Low-Bandwidth Access Channels

Potential future channels include:

* WhatsApp;
* SMS;
* USSD;
* voice interfaces;
* other lightweight access methods.

## 7. Evidence and Verification

Future versions could introduce:

* richer evidence comparison;
* source-change alerts;
* document monitoring;
* structured citation interfaces;
* stronger freshness indicators;
* more sophisticated claim analysis.

## 8. Transparency and Accountability Tools

Potential extensions include:

* public spending information;
* public-contract information;
* institutional decision tracking;
* policy change monitoring;
* public-document comparison.

## 9. Professional User Tools

Potential future experiences for journalists, researchers and civil-society organisations could include:

* evidence export;
* source comparison;
* document collections;
* structured research views;
* civic datasets.

## 10. Production Readiness

Before production deployment, CivicTrust would require additional work in areas including:

* security;
* privacy governance;
* accessibility testing;
* source governance;
* data retention;
* monitoring;
* moderation;
* reliability;
* legal/compliance review;
* third-party integration governance.

## Guiding Principle

Future development should preserve the core idea:

**Find reliable information.
Understand what it means.
Verify what you hear.
Know what to do next.**
