# CivicTrust — Demo Script

**Target:** 5–10 minutes

## Opening — The Problem

> “Civic information is everywhere, but finding information you can trust, understanding it, verifying what we hear and knowing what to do next can be difficult.”

## Introduce CivicTrust

> “CivicTrust is an AI-assisted civic information and action platform that helps people find, understand, verify and act on civic information they can trust.”

On-screen: **FIND → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → ACT**

## Ask CivicTrust

> “A citizen can ask a civic question in plain language. CivicTrust helps turn that question into an understandable answer while connecting the response to available evidence and sources.”

## Evidence

> “The evidence is not hidden. Users can inspect the source and understand why it was used.”

On-screen: **Evidence Before Confidence**

## Verify a Claim

> “Users can also submit a civic claim for verification. CivicTrust compares the claim with available information and presents the result together with supporting evidence and context.”

## Report / Safety

> “CivicTrust also provides a pathway for citizens to record civic, public-service and relevant safety concerns, including privacy-conscious reporting where appropriate.”

## Community / Action

> “The goal is not simply to provide information. CivicTrust helps people understand what they can legitimately do next.”

## Closing

> “CivicTrust doesn't ask people to trust the AI. It shows them the evidence.”

On-screen: **CivicTrust — Evidence Before Confidence**

**Find. Understand. Verify. Act.**
