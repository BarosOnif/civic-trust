# CivicTrust — Master AI App-Build Prompt

Build **CivicTrust — Evidence Before Confidence**, an AI-assisted civic information and action platform that helps people:

**FIND → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → ACT**

The initial focus is Nigeria. This is an invention-sprint proof of concept, not a production-ready national civic platform.

## Do Not Change the Concept

Do not turn CivicTrust into a generic chatbot, social network, news aggregator, political campaign tool, government website clone, candidate recommendation platform or generic complaint-management system.

## Primary User

The primary user is the ordinary citizen. The product should help users find civic information, understand policies/documents, verify claims, identify responsible institutions and know an appropriate next step.

## Core Modules

1. Ask CivicTrust
2. Verify a Claim
3. Evidence & Sources
4. Document Explainer
5. Civic Search / Explore
6. Civic Action / Next Step
7. Report an Issue
8. Safety & Protection
9. Community & Social Cohesion
10. Source Directory
11. Admin Dashboard
12. Feedback

## Trust Principle

> “We don't ask you to trust the AI. We show you the evidence.”

## Source Priority

Prefer official government sources, public institutions, public records/legal sources, research/civil-society sources, reputable media, then user-submitted information.

## Verification Statuses

Supported, Contradicted, Partially Supported, Not Supported, Outdated, Unverified, Needs Context.

## AI Guardrails

Never fabricate sources, laws, institutions, statistics, quotations, evidence, contact information or completed external actions.

If evidence is insufficient, say:

> “I couldn't verify this from the available sources.”

If sources disagree, show the disagreement.

## Privacy

Do not unnecessarily request NIN, BVN, bank information, passwords or other sensitive information. Support anonymous reporting where appropriate.

## Political Neutrality

Do not rank, score or recommend political candidates; tell users how to vote; create targeted political persuasion; or predict election winners.

## Real-World Conditions

Design for mobile-first access, low bandwidth, accessibility, privacy, multilingual readiness and local relevance.

## POC Priorities

Make these workflows functional:
1. Ask a civic question
2. Display a source-backed answer
3. Inspect evidence
4. Verify a claim
5. Report an issue
6. Review a report through admin
7. Identify a legitimate next step

Clearly label demonstration data and simulated integrations. Do not claim external actions were completed unless a genuine integration exists.
