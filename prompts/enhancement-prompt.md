# CivicTrust — POC Enhancement Prompt

Enhance the existing CivicTrust POC.

**Do not rebuild from scratch. Do not remove working features. Do not change the core product concept.**

The current strong area is **Transparency & Accountability**. Make the other two challenge tracks more explicit:

- Safety, Reporting & Protection
- Stability & Social Cohesion

## Preserve

Preserve Ask CivicTrust, Verify a Claim, Evidence/Source view, Document Explainer, Explore, Report an Issue, Source Directory, Admin Dashboard, visual identity and navigation.

## Safety & Protection

Create a visible Safety & Protection entry point and support:
- Threat or Intimidation
- Violence
- Abuse
- Harassment
- Public Safety Concern
- Community Safety Concern
- Other Safety Concern

Ask:
> “Are you in immediate danger?”

For immediate danger, provide only verified emergency/support information where available. Never fabricate emergency contacts. For non-immediate situations, provide reporting/information pathways.

Extend the existing Report an Issue flow to support safety reports. Support anonymous reporting where appropriate.

Explicitly communicate:
> “CivicTrust is not an emergency response service.”

Never claim an external report was submitted unless a genuine integration completed it.

## Community & Social Cohesion

Create a Community & Social Cohesion area supporting community concerns, local-service issues, neighbourhood/infrastructure concerns, environmental concerns, community dispute information, public-space concerns and local civic information.

Provide:
**Understand → Find Information → Check Evidence → Identify Responsible Institution → Act**

Do not turn CivicTrust into a social network or professional mediation service.

## Strengthen Claim Verification

Support community misinformation/context use cases, show claim, status, evidence, sources, dates/context and uncertainty.

## Admin Enhancements

Add/extend Safety Reports, Community Reports, category filtering, status updates, internal notes and audit visibility.

## Final Position

The enhanced POC should demonstrate:
- Transparency & Accountability — Strong
- Safety, Reporting & Protection — Clearly visible
- Stability & Social Cohesion — Clearly visible

All three should feel like connected applications of one CivicTrust concept.
